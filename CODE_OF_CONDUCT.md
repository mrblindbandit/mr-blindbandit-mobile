# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone contributing to
Blindbandit Records projects — regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, experience level, nationality, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not people
- Respect privacy and accessibility needs
- Do not share others’ personal data or credentials

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, trolling, hate speech, sexualized language/imagery in project
spaces, doxxing, or publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **business@mrblindbandit.net**.  
Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or ban contributors
who violate this code.

## Scope

Applies to GitHub repositories, issues, PRs, discussions, and official Blindbandit
community channels tied to this project.
