Version 2.3.4 (52)

- Play voice messages sent by current and older app versions, including legacy clear M4A attachments.
- Confirm voice messages no longer fail with missingKey when the file is playable without encryption metadata.
- Confirm incoming encrypted messages never display raw JSON, attachment keys, call-frame keys, or nonces.
- Start and answer voice/video calls; call-key control traffic must not create a visible notification.
- Confirm actual incoming calls still show a human-readable caller message and open the call screen.
