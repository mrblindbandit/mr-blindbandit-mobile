2.3.0 (48) — Agora call audio: stereo, media volume, spatial seats

• Agora calls use the highest music stereo profile (48 kHz, stereo) and the media volume, not the phone-call volume.
• Noise suppression is off, including AI noise suppression, so the room stays audible. Echo cancellation stays on.
• People are seated around you. A one-to-one call places the other person in front. VoiceOver says where they are, for example "Emma joined, on your left."
• Wired headphones and Bluetooth turn on a headphone equalizer. Speaker and earpiece do not.
• A group call stays open for two minutes after the last other person leaves.

Please try with VoiceOver, on a real Agora call:
1. Speaker, then earpiece.
2. Wired headphones. Hear the route announcement and confirm the other person is still audible.
3. Bluetooth headphones. Same check. Switch back to the speaker.
4. One-to-one: the other person is announced in front of you. Background sound in their room should still be audible, without a strong echo on speakerphone.
5. If a third person joins, each person gets a spoken seat. When someone leaves, the others are still audible.
