2.0.6 (21): Screen sharing now sends sound. When you share your screen in a call, the other person hears whatever is playing on your iPhone (videos, music, other apps) along with your voice. Muting yourself while sharing mutes only your voice; the shared sound keeps going. Please test: start a call, Share screen, Start Broadcast, play a YouTube video or a song, and ask the other person if they hear it; then mute and unmute. Note: some apps (Apple Music, Netflix, Spotify and other protected content) block their audio from screen recording, so it will not be heard. VoiceOver speech is usually not sent. Voice messages fixed: with VoiceOver, double tap a voice message (yours or theirs) to play, double tap again to pause; speed and skip are in the rotor. If one won't play, the reason is shown in Settings, AI, Voice message debug log. Encryption keys now set up on every iPhone (before, they never did): open the app, sign in, and you will be asked to "Create your encryption passcode" (6 or more digits or letters). Keep it: it restores your keys on a new iPhone so your security number stays the same; "Forgot passcode" makes new keys. Please test: open a chat, tap the name, Verify safety number. Once both of you have 2.0.6 you see 60 digits (swipe to the top line to hear all 12 groups with pauses), a QR code, Scan, a "Mark as verified" switch, Copy and Share. Before that it says the other person needs to open the latest app. Calls are now end-to-end encrypted when both people have 2.0.6: the call screen says "End-to-end encrypted"; with an older app it says "Not encrypted: other person needs to update". Group info has "Security numbers for each member". Calls: pressing Call should show the call screen right away. After you hang up, you should not get called back by the same call. New default ringtone "Mr. BlindBandit": incoming calls ring with it; Settings, Ringtone lists it first with a preview, plus "Mr. BlindBandit Classic", and a tone you picked before stays.
