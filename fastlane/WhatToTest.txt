2.0.6 (21): Screen sharing now sends sound. When you share your screen in a call, the other person hears whatever is playing on your iPhone (videos, music, other apps) along with your voice. Muting yourself while sharing mutes only your voice; the shared sound keeps going. Please test: start a call, Share screen, Start Broadcast, play a YouTube video or a song, and ask the other person if they hear it; then mute and unmute. Note: some apps (Apple Music, Netflix, Spotify and other protected content) block their audio from screen recording, so it will not be heard. VoiceOver speech is usually not sent. Voice messages fixed: with VoiceOver, double tap a voice message (yours or theirs) to play, double tap again to pause; speed and skip are in the rotor. If one won't play, the reason is shown in Settings, AI, Voice message debug log. Encryption keys now set up on every iPhone (before, they never did): open the app, sign in, and you will be asked to "Create your encryption passcode" (6 or more digits or letters). Keep it: it restores your keys on a new iPhone so your security number stays the same; "Forgot passcode" makes new keys. Please test: open a chat, tap the name, Verify safety number. Once both of you have 2.0.6 you see 60 digits (swipe to the top line to hear all 12 groups with pauses), a QR code, Scan, a "Mark as verified" switch, Copy and Share. Before that it says the other person needs to open the latest app. Calls are now end-to-end encrypted when both people have 2.0.6: the call screen says "End-to-end encrypted"; with an older app it says "Not encrypted: other person needs to update". Group info has "Security numbers for each member".

2.0.5 (20): Calls fixed. Answering a call now connects instead of failing, and the caller no longer gets hung up on right away. Incoming calls ring only on the phone of the person being called. Please test: call someone, let them answer, talk for a minute; also try declining and letting it ring out.

2.0.4 (19): Fixed Apple Music in the AI chat answering "sorry, I can't play that". Saying "play Blinding Lights", "play Blinding Lights by The Weeknd", "play the album After Hours", "pause", "skip", "previous" or "shuffle my library" now goes straight to Apple Music without waiting on the AI. If Apple Music access is off, it tells you exactly where to turn it on. Without a subscription you get 30-second previews. If something fails, the real error is spoken and shown in Settings, AI, Music debug log.

2.0.3 (18): Apple Music in the AI chat: ask "play Drake", "play my library on shuffle", "queue Blinding Lights", "pause", "next" or "what's playing". It plays inside the app, and a Now Playing bar at the top of the AI chat has Play/Pause, Next and Previous (rotor actions too). Without an Apple Music subscription you get 30-second previews. The AI can search the web: answers include links, and double tapping a link (or the rotor action "Open link") opens it inside the app. Turn it off with Settings, AI, Allow AI web search; only the search words go through mrblindbandit.net, never your chats. Cloud AI (off by default): Settings, AI, Cloud AI turns on the bigger AI for hard questions (or rotor "Ask the bigger AI"), "Make an image", Whisper transcripts for long voice notes (rotor "Show transcript"), Bisaya/Tagalog translation, summaries and "Check link safety"; it shows replies and images left today and falls back to on-device AI when it runs out. Owner account: Settings, AI, Style (Hood or Professional) and an Unfiltered switch; public @AI answers stay Professional. Faster: chats open instantly from cache, sent messages appear right away with "Sending…", AI warms up when you start typing, haptics on send and receive, calls connect faster.

2.0.2 (17): AI chat backup: Settings, Security, AI chat backup. Back up and restore your AI chat and what it remembers, end-to-end encrypted; a new iPhone restores it automatically. Chat settings now follow the server: Allow AI in this chat (admins set it for groups; when off, summaries, smart replies and @AI stay off), screenshot alerts, view-once, relay-only, only admins can post, approve new members, and a new Mute notification sound switch. Pin and Unpin on any message now say "Pinned" or "Unpinned"; if the server isn't ready yet it keeps the pin on this iPhone and tells you. Recovery passphrase backup works with the live server's format. Revoking an invite link shows "Needs server update" until the server supports it.
