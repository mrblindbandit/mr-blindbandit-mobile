2.3.2 (50) — Voice-message playback repair

• New encrypted voice messages bind their uploaded audio to the sent message so it is not treated as an abandoned temporary upload.
• Legacy clear-m4a voice messages can play even when old key metadata is absent.
• Standard base64, base64url, and legacy key/nonce field names are accepted.

Please send and play a voice message in both a direct chat and a group. Also retry the message that previously announced “Attachment key or nonce missing.”
